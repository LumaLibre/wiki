The most widely available high-tier items are the Astral gear sets; Sought after for both their unique strengths and their collectability, these items come in many forms with many different enchantments. Astral Orbs can be acquired though crates or by crafting them from their core as obtained from pinatas.

There are currently <Badge text="11" variant="caution" size="small" style={{ fontWeight: 'bold' }}/> obtainable Astral set types.
