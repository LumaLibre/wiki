LumaMC Features a robust economy system where players can utilize currency (known as 'Money') to trade with others, pay the taxes on their town plot, or interact with chest shops!

### How to check your balance

Use `/bal` to view your current balance.

### How to view the balance leaderboard

Use `/baltop` to view a leaderboard of players with the top balances in the server.

### How to use the pay system

<div class="list">

    - `/pay <player>` - Transfers Money from your balance to the other player's balance.
    - `/payoff`  - Disables incoming payments from other players.
    - `/payon` - Enables incoming payments from other players.
    - `/payconfirm` - Enables/Disables payment confirmation when paying other players.

</div>
