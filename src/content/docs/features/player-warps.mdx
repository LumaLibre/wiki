## Player Warps

<Aside>
  To create your own pwarp, you must be at least *Quantum* rank!
</Aside>

The player warp GUI is accessible with the `/pw` command. It allows players to browse and search public warps to find shops, farms, art galleries, and more!
You may also skip the navigation GUI and teleport directly to a pwarp that you know the name of with `/pw [warpname]`.

<CenteredImage src={pwGUI} alt="Player Warp GUI" tailwind="w-82" />

## Player Warps GUI

The `/pw` navigation UI provides a convenient resource for the advertisement and access of player-created warps. It contains several key features:

<div class="list">
- All Warps | Browse a list of all publicly available player warps
- Favorites | View your own personal collection of favorited pwarps
- Visited Warps | View a list of your last visted pwarps
- Categories | See a sorted list of pwarps based on if they are a store (chest), a farm (carrot), or an art display (painting).
- Advertising | Pay in-game money to advertise your warp on the front menu of `/pw` for up to 2 days!
</div>

You can right-click on any of the warps displayed to favorite them, or even rate them from 1-5 stars!

## Creating your own Warps

Having warps to go to requires someone to make them, right?
Once you've reached **Quantum** rank, you can create your own warp using `/pw create [warpname]`.
After being set, your warp can be removed with `/pw remove [warpname]`
You are limited to one owned pwarp at first, increasing with rank as such:

<div class="list">
- **Quantum - Interlude** | 1 PWarp
- **Revelation - Stellaris** | 3 Pwarps
- **Cosmos - Serene** | 4 Pwarps
- **Singularity - Odyssey** | 6 Pwarps
- **Eclipsed** | 7 Pwarps
- **Maven** | 16 Pwarps
</div>

A variety of additional commands can help you modify and publicize your warps. Some of the most used include:

<div class="list">
- `/pw set [warpname]` | Changes an existing warp to your current location
- `/pw desc set/remove [warpname] [description]` | Adds a description to your warp as viewable in the /pw menu
- `/pw icon set/remove [warpname]` | Sets the icon of your warp to the item in your hand, as viewable in the /pw menu
- `/pw category set/remove [warpname] [shops/farms/art]` | Adds or removes your warp from one of the 3 categories viewable in the /pw menu
</div>
