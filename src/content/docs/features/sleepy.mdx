By playing on LumaMC, you will slowly accumulate a currency called "Oneira". Oneira can be spent at the **Dream Market** for unique cosmetic items.
The Dream Market is a **cosmetic only**, rotating store that may be present on LumaMC for only a few months out of the year. Cosmetics in this shop may be from old events or brand new! The items in this shop will rotate out after about a month or so.
<CenteredImage src={dreammarket} alt="In-Game Dream Market" tailwind="w-140" />
*Hint: you can visit the Dream Market by teleporting to the AFK area.*
### Common Commands:

<div class="list">

    - `/afk` - Sets your status to "Away From Keyboard".
    - `/afk warp` - Teleports you to the AFK area.
    - `/playtime`  - Displays the total time you have spent playing on LumaMC.
    - `/playtime <player>` - Displays the specified player's time spent playing on LumaMC.
    - `/sleepy afktime` - Displays the total time you have been AFK on LumaMC.
    - `/sleepy points` - Displays the number of Oneira you currently have.
    - `/sleepy oneira` - Displays the number of Oneira you currently have.
</div>
